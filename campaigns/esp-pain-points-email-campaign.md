**Target segment:** Shopify merchants on Klaviyo, Omnisend, Postscript, or Attentive showing
plateaued email/SMS revenue, low campaign-send cadence, or heavy reliance on list-wide blasts
instead of segmented flows — i.e., merchants living the pain the reviews describe, not just
"anyone using an ESP."
**Goal:** Book a demo / start a free trial of LTV.ai as the "autonomous" layer that replaces the
manual work their current ESP still requires.

never bashes a specific competitor by name in the subject line (use "your ESP" / "your current
tool"), and closes with the same low-friction CTA.

## Notes for whoever sends this

- Do not name competitors in subject lines or preview text (deliverability + it reads as
  insecure marketing). Naming them once in body copy, factually, is fine — the emails above do
  this sparingly.
- Segment eligibility should be re-checked before each send (plateaued revenue / low cadence /
  blast-heavy sending), not just "uses one of these four apps" — using the pain signal, not just
  the tool, keeps this from reading as a mass competitor-conquest blast.
- Suggested spacing: Day 0, Day 3, Day 6, Day 10. Stop the sequence on any reply or booked demo.
